About

I study architecture at UBT in Prishtinë. My work sits between the building, the room and the object — how light enters, what a material does up close, and how a space feels when you walk in.

Beside studio courses I run ArchiMuse: a quiet place to show interiors, furniture studies and house concepts. I draw, model and also design the way the work is presented online. I am looking for a first studio role or internship where I can learn from built work.

How I work

  • Start from the site and the brief — not from a style first.
  • Test space with sketches, simple models and references.
  • Keep materials honest: wood, stone, linen, measured light.
  • Present the idea so a client can read it without noise.
